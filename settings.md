# Settings

Open **Settings** from the bottom bar, the side menu or the pink circle at the top of the home
screen.

![Settings](https://getsettalk.github.io/toonik-docs/screenshots/settings-1.webp)

At the top you can **Rate us** and **Share app**.

## Appearance

- **Theme** — System, Light or Dark.
- **Status bar icons** — Auto (dark or light, whichever shows on the canvas), always dark or always
  light.
- **Hide status bar while drawing** — more room for the canvas. Swipe down from the top to see it for
  a moment.

## Drawing

![Drawing and canvas settings](https://getsettalk.github.io/toonik-docs/screenshots/settings-2.webp)

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Stylus pressure** | Pressing harder with a pen draws thicker | On |
| **Touch sensitivity** | Soft, Medium or Firm — how hard you need to press | Medium |
| **Minimum pressure** | The lightest a pen stroke can be | 12% |
| **Draw input** | Stylus and touch, or **Stylus only** (fingers then only zoom and move) | Stylus and touch |
| **Stylus hover preview** | Shows the brush or eraser size as a circle while a pen hovers over the screen | Off |
| **Eraser outline** | Shows the eraser's size as a circle while you erase | On |

## Canvas & gestures

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Smooth zoom** | Lines stay crisp when you zoom in close (Android 13+). Turn off to see the real pixels | On |
| **Canvas rotation** | Twist with two fingers to rotate the canvas | On |
| **Tap gestures** | Two-finger tap to undo, three-finger tap to redo | On |
| **Haptic feedback** | Small vibrations on some buttons | On |
| **Left-handed toolbar** | Moves the tool options pill to the left edge | Off |
| **Keep screen on** | The screen stays on while a project is open | On |

## Sound

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Sound while animating** | Each frame's sound plays as you step through frames, a note marks the frames a beat falls on, and the sound wave stays up while playing (see [Sound](https://getsettalk.github.io/toonik-docs/sound.md)) | Off |

## Projects

![Project and about settings](https://getsettalk.github.io/toonik-docs/screenshots/settings-3.webp)

- **Default FPS** — the speed new projects start with (12 fps).
- **Autosave** — how often your work is saved while you draw: every 5, 10 (default) or 30 seconds,
  every minute or every 2 minutes.

## About

- **Open-source licences** — credits for the parts Toonik is built with, including the sound
  library.
- **Reset settings** — puts every setting back to its default. Your projects and movies are not
  touched.
