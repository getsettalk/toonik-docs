# The editor

This is where you draw. Here is what every part does.

![The editor](https://getsettalk.github.io/toonik-docs/screenshots/editor.webp)

## Top bar

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **✕** | Saves and closes the project |
| **Pink diamond** | Opens the **Object library** to add a ready-made character or one of your saved objects |
| **TIMELINE** | Opens the full timeline (see *Frames and timeline*) |
| **Hide interface** (the square icon) | Hides the bars so you see more of your drawing |
| **Pink share arrow** | **Make movie** — export your animation |
| **⋮** | The project menu: add images, audio, stickers, onion skin, grid, project settings and more |

## The toolbar

The white bar near the top holds your tools: **Brush**, **Eraser**, **Lasso**, **Fill**, one more
tool that changes to the last one you used (Text at first), and **⋯** for the rest (**Text**,
**Smudge**, **Blur**, **Magic cut**, **Eyedropper**).

![More tools](https://getsettalk.github.io/toonik-docs/screenshots/more-tools.webp)

You can put the toolbar anywhere:

- **Drag the dotted grip** on its left end to move it.
- **Tap the grip** to turn it between lying flat and standing up.
- **Drop it at the side** of the screen and it stands up along the edge.
- **Drop it down by the frames** and it folds up to just the current tool and an arrow — tap either
  to see every tool in a column. Drag it back up to unfold it.

![Folded toolbar with the tool list open](https://getsettalk.github.io/toonik-docs/screenshots/toolbar-collapsed.webp)

## The tool options pill

The pill on the right edge changes with the tool. For the brush it shows **Brush** (choose a brush),
**Size**, **Opacity**, **Color** and **Ruler**.

- **Drag up or down** on a control to change it — a bubble shows the value.
- **Tap or long-press** a control to open all the settings for that tool.
- **Tap Color** to open the colour picker.
- Drag its top grip to move the pill anywhere. Let go near a side and it stands against that side.
  (**Left-handed toolbar** in Settings starts it on the left edge.)
- Let go of it down by the timeline and it folds to its main controls (**Size** and **Color** for the
  brush) and an arrow. Tap the arrow to open the rest upwards, and again to fold them. Drag it back up
  to unfold it.

## The bottom bar

**Audio**, **Image/Video**, **Undo**, **Redo**, **Copy**, **Paste** and **Layer** (the pink number is
the layer you are drawing on). The frames of your animation sit just above it.

- **Copy** copies what you have selected, or the whole layer if nothing is selected.
- **Paste** puts it back as a selection in **the same spot** — handy for carrying a drawing to the
  next frame.

## Gestures on the canvas

| Gesture | Does |
|---|---|
| One finger | Draws with the current tool |
| Two fingers pinch | Zoom in and out |
| Two fingers drag | Move around the canvas |
| Two fingers twist | Rotate the canvas (can be turned off in Settings) |
| Two-finger tap | **Undo** |
| Three-finger tap | **Redo** |
| Any tap while playing | Stops playback |

## Smooth zoom

Zoom in close and your lines stay crisp instead of turning into blurry blocks of pixels. Soft
brushes stay soft. Only how it looks on screen changes — your drawing and your movies are exactly
the same. Needs Android 13 or newer; turn it off in **Settings → Canvas & gestures → Smooth zoom**
if you want to see the real pixels (for pixel art).

![Smooth zoom off and on](https://getsettalk.github.io/toonik-docs/screenshots/smooth-zoom-compare.webp)

## Hide interface

Tap the **hide interface** button to see more of your drawing. A small set of controls stays: your
current tool, **Show** (brings everything back), **Copy**, **Paste**, play, the frame numbers and
**Layers**.

![Interface hidden](https://getsettalk.github.io/toonik-docs/screenshots/hide-interface.webp)

## The project menu (⋮)

![The project menu](https://getsettalk.github.io/toonik-docs/screenshots/stage-menu.webp)

At the top you see the project's size, speed and number of frames. Then:

- **Add image**, **Add video**, **Audio**, **Objects**
- **Make sticker**, **Save as object**, **Frames** (all frames in a grid), **Help & tips**
- **Onion skin** and **Grid** — switch them on or off here; **Edit** changes their settings
- **Project settings** — name, speed, timing, drawing outside the canvas and background
- **Make movie**

## Project settings

![Project settings](https://getsettalk.github.io/toonik-docs/screenshots/project-settings.webp)

- **Project name**
- **Frames per second** — the speed of the whole animation (1 to 60)
- **Timing** — hold every drawing for 1, 2 or 3 frames (**On ones / On twos / On threes**)
- **Canvas size** — shown here, but fixed once the project exists
- **Draw outside the canvas** — room around the canvas to draw in, and **Room size** to set how much
  (see *Images, video and objects*)
- **Background** — presets, a colour or a picture
- **Stage background** — the colour around the canvas while you draw (not in your movie)

Tap **✓** to save or **✕** to close without saving.

## Leaving the editor

**✕** or your phone's back button saves and closes. The back button first closes whatever is open
(a menu, a sheet, a selection), so press it again to leave.
