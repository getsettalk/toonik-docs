Getting started ​

Toonik is a frame-by-frame animation app. You draw a picture, add a new frame, draw it again a little differently, and when the frames play one after another your drawing moves. Everything in the app is free, there is no account to create and no ads.

The home screen ​

  • Projects and Movies — the two tabs. Swipe left or right to switch.
  • ☰ (menu) — opens the side menu (see below). A pink dot means there is something new to read.
  • 🔔 (bell) — your notifications from Toonik. A number shows how many are unread.
  • Pink circle (top right) — opens Settings.
  • Bottom bar — Home, Objects (the object library), + (new project), Learn (short animation lessons) and Settings.

Tap a project to open it. The small labels on each card show how long the animation is and its frames per second (fps).

The side menu ​

App settings, Object library, How to animate (lessons), What's new (what each update brought), Notifications, About, Rate this app and Share Toonik.

Create a project ​

Tap the pink + at the bottom.

  1. Project name — type a name, or keep "My Animation 1".

  2. Choose background — the four buttons under the preview:

    • Presets — paper, white, grid, lined, dot grid, sky, sunset, night sky, meadow, green screen, black and more.
    • Camera — take a photo and draw over it.
    • Gallery — use a picture from your phone.
    • Color — a plain colour (pick a swatch or type a HEX code).

  3. Choose canvas size — ready-made sizes for YouTube, YouTube Shorts, Instagram, TikTok, stickers and more, or type your own width and height (64 to 2048 pixels).

  4. Choose frames per second — how many drawings play each second. 12 fps is a good start. The bouncing ball shows how fast it will look.

  5. Tap Create project.

Good to know: the canvas size cannot be changed after the project is created. The name, speed and background can be changed any time in Project settings.

Work with your projects ​

Long-press a project to select it. Tap more cards to select several.

The bar at the bottom then lets you:

  • ✏️ Rename (one project at a time)
  • ⧉ Duplicate — makes a copy called "… copy"
  • Share / Export project — saves the whole project as a .flippa file you can send, back up or open on another phone
  • 🗑 Delete — asks first, and cannot be undone

Tap ✕ at the top to leave selection mode.

Sort, import and select ​

Tap ⋮ next to the tabs.

  • Sort by — Last modified, Name or Date created.
  • Import project — open a Toonik backup (.flippa) or a FlipaClip project (.fc). It is added as a new project; nothing you already have is replaced. From FlipaClip, the layers and drawings come across (layer effects and sound do not).
  • Select — start selection mode.

Time-lapse recording ​

Toonik can record every stroke you draw and turn it into a time-lapse video of your drawing. Select a project, tap ⋯ at the top, and switch on Time-Lapse Recording. Later, use Share Time-Lapse from the same place. Time-lapse videos also appear in the Movies tab.

Your work is saved automatically ​

Toonik saves while you draw (every 10 seconds by default — you can change this in Settings) and again when you leave the editor or switch to another app. Your projects stay on your phone; nothing you draw is uploaded anywhere.

What's new and notifications ​

What's new (side menu) lists every update and what it changed. The bell keeps the latest 15 messages from Toonik, even ones you missed. Tap a message to open its link.
