Select, move, crop and animate ​

Select with the lasso ​

  1. Choose Lasso in the toolbar.
  2. Draw a loop around what you want. It doesn't have to be neat — Toonik picks up just the drawing inside the loop and fits the box tightly around it.
  3. The drawing now floats in a box you can change.

Tip: double-tap the Lasso tool to select everything on the layer.

The box ​

PartWhat to do
Inside the boxDrag to move it
White round cornersDrag to make it bigger or smaller (keeps its shape)
White bars on the edgesDrag to stretch it wider or taller
↻ button below the boxDrag around to rotate. It clicks into place at every 15°
⋯ button above the boxMore options (see below)
Two fingers on the boxPinch to resize and twist to rotate

Tap outside the box to put the drawing down. Press Back to cancel and leave everything as it was.

You can move a selection into the room around the canvas too (see Images, video and objects).

The bar under the box ​

Trsf (move, resize, rotate), Persp (drag each corner freely for a perspective look), Move (exact placement, below), Flip (left–right), Flip (up–down), Crop, Animate and Erase (delete what is selected).

Move it exactly ​

When a finger is not precise enough, tap Move. Arrows appear above the bar:

  • Tap an arrow to move the selection one step, or hold it to keep moving.
  • X and Y show where the top left of the box is on the canvas, in pixels.
  • The button on the right switches the step between 1 px and 10 px.

The ⋯ menu ​

Choose Transform or Perspective, Add to selection or Remove from selection, or Crop, Flip horizontally, Flip vertically and Delete.

Add to or remove from a selection ​

Tap Add to selection or Remove from selection in the ⋯ menu. The box steps aside and the selection shows as a dotted outline. Now every loop you draw is added to it (Add), or cut out of it (Remove) — switch between the two on the bar below. Draw as many loops as you like; pinch to zoom in for small parts. Tap Done (or Back) and the box comes back around everything selected, ready to move, flip or animate.

Crop ​

Trim a picture you added, or anything you selected.

  • Drag the corners and edges, or drag inside to move the crop box.
  • Pick a shape: Free, 1:1, 4:3, 3:4, 16:9 or 9:16.
  • Trim edges cuts away empty, see-through margins in one tap.
  • Reset starts again. Apply keeps the cropped part exactly where it was, still selected.

Animate a selection ​

Turn the selected drawing into a movement in one go — Toonik draws all the frames for you.

  1. Tap Animate.
  2. Pick a Preset (Move left to right, Bounce, Spin, Grow, Shrink, Fade in, Fade out, Shake, Float…), or set it up yourself: choose Start or End, then set Position (drag the S and E handles on the preview, or use X and Y: the slider gets close, − and + move one pixel, and holding them keeps going), Scale, Rotation and Opacity. To place a handle precisely, tap the magnifier in the corner of the preview for a close-up of it (tap again to see the whole stage), or pinch the preview with two fingers; one finger moves around while zoomed in.
  3. Under Timing, choose how many Frames it lasts, the Easing (Linear, Ease in, Ease out, Bounce, Elastic…), how many times to Repeat, and Back and forth.
  4. Tap Create animation. The movement is put on a new layer called "Animation".

Copy to another frame ​

  1. Select the drawing with the lasso.
  2. Tap Copy in the bottom bar.
  3. Go to another frame and tap Paste. The copy appears in the same spot, still selected, so you can nudge it before putting it down.

Save as an object ​

Keep a drawing to use again in any project: open ⋮ → Save as object, choose Selection, Current layer or Animation (a range of frames), give it a name and tags, and tap Save object. It appears in the Object library.
