# Tips, shortcuts and questions

## Lessons inside the app

**Learn** (bottom bar on the home screen) has short lessons: frame-by-frame basics, frames per
second, onion skin, layers, lasso and copy-paste, ruler and mirror, audio, making a movie and the
object library. Tap a lesson to open its steps.

![How to animate](https://getsettalk.github.io/toonik-docs/screenshots/learn.webp)

In the editor, **⋮ → Help & tips** explains every tool in a sentence or two.

![Help and tips](https://getsettalk.github.io/toonik-docs/screenshots/help-tips.webp)

## Handy shortcuts

- **Double-tap Brush** — ruler on or off.
- **Double-tap Lasso** — select everything on the layer.
- **Two-finger tap** — undo. **Three-finger tap** — redo.
- **Long-press a frame** — hold, duplicate, copy, paste, move, share.
- **Long-press any button** — shows its name.
- **Copy, then Paste on another frame** — the drawing lands in the same spot.

## Keyboard (tablets and phones with a keyboard)

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| ← / → | Previous / next frame |
| ↑ / ↓ | Layer above / below |
| Space | Play / stop |
| O | Onion skin on / off |
| B | Brush |
| E | Eraser |
| Ctrl + Z | Undo |
| Ctrl + Shift + Z or Ctrl + Y | Redo |

## Questions

**Can I change the canvas size later?**
No — choose it when you create the project. The speed, timing, name and background can change any
time.

**Why can't I add another layer?**
A project can have up to 15 layers. Merge two layers (swipe a layer to the left and tap **Merge**) or delete one
you no longer need.

**My sticker pack has no "Add to WhatsApp" button.**
It does, but it stays faded until the pack has 3 stickers — WhatsApp won't take fewer. Make more
stickers into the same pack.

**Something I moved off the canvas disappeared.**
In a project without room around the canvas, whatever is outside the canvas is cut when you put it
down. Turn on **Project settings → Draw outside the canvas** to have room around it (new projects
have it already).

**Smooth zoom makes no difference on my phone.**
It needs Android 13 or newer, and it only shows when you zoom in close. Check that **Settings →
Smooth zoom** is on.

**Where are my movies?**
In the **Movies** tab of the home screen. Use **Save to gallery** to put a copy in your phone's
gallery.

**How do I move my projects to a new phone?**
Long-press a project → **Export project**, send the `.flippa` file to the new phone, and open it with
**⋮ → Import project**.

**Can I open my FlipaClip projects?**
Yes. Export the project from FlipaClip as a `.fc` file, then in Toonik use **⋮ → Import project**.
The drawings and layers come across.

**Do I need an account? Does anything I draw get uploaded?**
No account is needed, and your drawings stay on your phone. Everything in Toonik is free.
