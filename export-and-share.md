# Make a movie, stickers and sharing

## Make a movie

Tap the **pink share arrow** at the top of the editor, or **Make movie** in the **⋮** menu.

![Make movie](https://getsettalk.github.io/toonik-docs/screenshots/make-movie.webp)

- **Movie name**
- **Output size** — **Original** (the canvas size), **1080p**, **720p**, **480p**, **Square 1080**,
  **Vertical** (1080 × 1920) or **Custom**.
- **Format**:

  ![Formats](https://getsettalk.github.io/toonik-docs/screenshots/movie-format.webp)

  - **MP4** — a video with sound. Best for sharing on YouTube, Instagram, WhatsApp.
  - **GIF** — an animated picture. Can have a see-through background. Choose **Loop forever** and
    **Dithering** (smoother colours).
  - **PNG Sequence** — every frame as a picture in a zip file, for video editing apps.
- **Transparent background** — GIF and PNG only.
- **Watermark** — a small Toonik logo in the corner. Off unless you turn it on.
- **Include audio** — MP4 only, when the project has sound.

Tap **Make movie**. When it is ready you can **Share** it, **Save to gallery**, or tap **Done**.

![Your movie is ready](https://getsettalk.github.io/toonik-docs/screenshots/movie-ready.webp)

Your movies are kept in the **Movies** tab on the home screen. Tap one to play it, share it, save it
to your gallery or delete it.

![Movies tab](https://getsettalk.github.io/toonik-docs/screenshots/movies.webp)

![Playing a movie](https://getsettalk.github.io/toonik-docs/screenshots/movie-player.webp)

## Make a WhatsApp sticker

Open **⋮ → Make sticker**. Your animation becomes an animated WhatsApp sticker.

![Make a sticker](https://getsettalk.github.io/toonik-docs/screenshots/make-sticker.webp)

1. Pick up to three **Emoji** — WhatsApp suggests your sticker when someone types one of them.
2. Choose a **Pack**, or type a name to start a new one.
3. Tap **Make sticker**.

Toonik frames the sticker around your drawing, so even a small doodle fills it. Stickers are
512 × 512 and under 500 KB, so long animations may lose some frames.

**WhatsApp needs at least 3 stickers in a pack** before it can be added. Until then the pack says how
many more it needs. When it is ready, tap **Add to WhatsApp**. Packs already in WhatsApp show
**Added**; with WhatsApp Business installed too, you can also **Add to Business**. A pack holds up to
30 stickers.

## Share a single frame

Long-press a frame in the frame strip and tap **Share** to send that drawing as a picture.

## Share or back up a whole project

On the home screen, long-press a project and tap **Export project**. You get a `.flippa` file: send
it to a friend, keep it as a backup, or open it on another phone with **⋮ → Import project**.
