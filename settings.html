On this page

Settings ​

Open Settings from the bottom bar, the side menu or the pink circle at the top of the home screen.

At the top you can Rate us and Share app.

Appearance ​

  • Theme — System, Light or Dark.
  • Status bar icons — Auto (dark or light, whichever shows on the canvas), always dark or always light.
  • Hide status bar while drawing — more room for the canvas. Swipe down from the top to see it for a moment.

Projects ​

  • Default FPS — the speed new projects start with (12 fps).
  • Autosave — how often your work is saved while you draw: every 5, 10 (default) or 30 seconds, every minute or every 2 minutes.

About ​

  • Open-source licences — credits for the parts Toonik is built with, including the sound library.
  • Reset settings — puts every setting back to its default. Your projects and movies are not touched.
