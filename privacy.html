Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 November 2026

Toonik is a frame-by-frame animation app for Android. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your drawings never leave your device, and the only thing the app sends is anonymous usage statistics.

Your work stays on your device

Every drawing, project, animation, audio recording, saved object and brush you make in the brush maker is written to private storage on your own device. None of it is uploaded anywhere. We cannot see your work, and we run no servers that hold it.

Sound and voice stay on the device too. The sound library is part of the app, so picking a sound downloads nothing. Sounds you add are worked on right on your phone — to draw their wave and, with “Sound while animating” on, to find where the beats fall. The Voice maker turns your text into speech with the speech engine already on your phone; Toonik sends the text nowhere, though that engine is made by your phone's maker or Google and follows their policy.

Uninstalling Toonik deletes everything it stored, so export anything you want to keep first.

When you export a video, GIF, image sequence or project backup, or use the share button, that file goes wherever you send it — your gallery, your files, or an app you pick. Once it leaves Toonik, the privacy policy of that destination applies instead of this one.

What the app sends over the internet

Toonik holds the INTERNET permission and uses it for exactly four things, none of which involve anything you draw:

Anonymous usage analytics, through Google Firebase Analytics: which features get used and how often, so we know what to improve. That means events like “a movie was exported as MP4” or “a sticker was created” — never the drawing, never a project or layer name, never anything you type. Counts such as frame numbers are rounded into bands first. Firebase assigns a random app-instance identifier and records device model, OS version, country and language, under Google's Privacy Policy. These analytics are part of the app and have no switch in Settings. If you turned them off in an earlier version, that choice is kept, and collection stays off inside the SDK itself.

Update checks. Toonik asks the Play Store app whether a newer version exists so it can prompt you to update. That happens inside Google Play and we receive nothing from it.

The rating prompt, if you choose to leave a review — also handled by Google Play.

News and update notifications, through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging. The app joins one shared topic that every install receives, so Google gives the device a random messaging token to deliver to. We never see or store that token, and we cannot message any one person — only everyone at once. When an announcement comes with a picture, the app downloads that picture from the address in the message to show it. Messages you receive are kept in the app's inbox on your device. You can switch them off by turning off Toonik's notifications in Android's settings.

There is no advertising and no third-party tracking of any kind.

Permissions, and why each one exists

PermissionWhat it is forWhen it applies
Microphone Recording a voice-over or sound effect onto your animation's audio track. Only while you are actively recording. Recordings are saved into your project on the device and never transmitted.
Vibration Brief haptic feedback on some controls. Can be switched off in Settings.
Notifications Showing news and update announcements. Asked once on Android 13 and newer; you can say no, and change it any time in Android's settings.
Storage Saving an exported video or image to your gallery. Only when you export or save, and only on Android 9 and older — newer versions do not need it.

Toonik does not request access to your location, contacts, calendar, phone, or your photo library at large.

Camera, photos, videos and files. When you add a photo or video to your animation, use one as a canvas background or pick a picture as a brush tip, Toonik hands you Android's own photo picker, camera app or file picker. Only the photos, videos or files you choose come back (up to 30 photos at a time), and they are copied into your project — or, for a brush tip, into the app's brushes — on the device. Toonik never browses your gallery and holds no camera permission of its own.

Children

Toonik is suitable for all ages. There is no sign-up, no chat, no social feature, no advertising and no purchase flow that gathers information. The anonymous usage analytics described above collect nothing that identifies a person.

Your rights

We hold no account and no personal data about you — the analytics above are anonymous and cannot be traced back to a person, so there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, export or delete. Your work is yours and sits on your own device, where you can remove it whenever you like — project by project in the app, or all at once by uninstalling.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published here and the date at the top updated. If a future release ever begins collecting data, we will say so plainly before it does.
