Images, video, objects and drawing outside the canvas ​

Add an image or a video ​

Tap Image/Video in the bottom bar.

  • Photos — pick one picture, or several at once (up to 30).
  • Videos — turn a clip into frames.
  • Photo camera / Video camera — take a photo or record a clip right now.
  • File — from Google Drive, Downloads or any other app.

One picture lands on the canvas as a selection: move it, resize it, Crop it, then tap outside to put it down.

When you pick several pictures, Toonik asks:

  • One per frame — each picture on a frame of its own. Great for turning photos into a stop-motion animation.
  • All on this frame — side by side on the current frame, to arrange together.

Importing a video ​

  • Drag the ends of the bar to choose the part of the clip (Start and End).
  • Import as — Animation (many frames) or Single frame (one still picture).
  • Look — Original, Line art (turns the video into drawn lines; choose Clean, Medium or Rich detail) or Trace guide (a faint, locked layer with an empty "Ink" layer on top, so you can trace over the video).
  • Timing — On ones or On twos.

The video goes on a new layer, starting at the current frame. Long clips make many frames, so pick just the part you need.

Draw outside the canvas ​

New projects have room around the canvas: draw a character off-screen and walk it in, or draw a wide background for a camera move. Only what is inside the canvas ends up in your movie.

Zoom out to see how much room there is — the dashed line is its edge:

Turn it on or off in ⋮ → Project settings → Draw outside the canvas. Turning it off crops everything drawn outside the canvas from every frame (you will be asked first).

Make the room bigger or smaller ​

While it is on, Room size under the switch sets how much room there is, by its width and height:

  • Drag Width or Height, or tap − and + to move each side by 10 px. Automatic goes back to the size Toonik picks; Current to what you have now.
  • The preview shows your drawing staying where it is, the new edge in pink and, in red, anything that would be cut off. You are asked before anything is cut.
  • Each layer of each frame is one picture this size, so room uses memory: Memory shows how much. When one side reaches what your device allows, make the other smaller to go further. Phones and tablets with more memory get more room.
  • Tap Apply. Changing the room can't be undone and clears Undo.

The Object library ​

Ready-made drawings and animations you can drop into any project, plus everything you save yourself. Open it from the pink diamond in the editor or Objects on the home screen.

  • Built-in objects — walk and run cycles, a bouncing ball, a jumping person, a fox, a flapping bird, people and animals seen from the front, back and sides (dog, cat, bird, rabbit, panda, fish), trees, bamboo, rocks, clouds, sun, moon, stars, a house, a beach ball and a car.
  • Filters — All, Static, Animated, Favourites, Built-in. 🔍 searches by name or tag.
  • Tap an object for its details, to add it to your favourites, or to duplicate a built-in one so you can change it.

From the editor the library opens as Insert object — tap an object to add it:

When you add an object to a project you can:

  • Place — put one frame of it on the canvas as a selection.

  • Stamp (animated objects) — draw its whole animation across your frames, repeated as many times as you like, on the current layer or a new one.

  • Animate — move it across the canvas with a motion you choose, like Animate selection.

Save your own objects ​

Select a drawing with the lasso (or use a whole layer, or a range of frames), then ⋮ → Save as object. Give it a name and tags so it is easy to find later.
