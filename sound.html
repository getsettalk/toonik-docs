Sound ​

Tap Audio in the bottom bar to open the audio editor.

  • The preview at the top shows the frame under the blue playhead.
  • 🔊 mutes or unmutes all sound while you work.
  • ⏮ ▶ ⏭ — play from here, or jump to the start or end.
  • The four buttons on the right: Sound FX (opens the sound library), Voice maker, Record audio and Import audio (music, or the sound of a video file, from your phone).
  • There are 6 tracks, so sounds can play at the same time. Each track can be muted or locked.
  • Your frames run along the bottom, so you can line a sound up with the exact drawing.

New sounds are placed at the playhead. Drag a clip to move it, drag its ends to trim it, and pinch to zoom in for precise timing.

Clip actions ​

Long-press a clip for Rename, Duplicate, Split at playhead, Volume and Delete.

The sound library ​

169 sounds, all free to use in your animations, in themed packs: Toonik basics, Fight, Cartoon & game, Footsteps & moves, Home & objects, Sci-fi & machines, Clicks & alerts, Music jingles and Voices (an announcer saying "3, 2, 1, Go!", "You win", "Game over"…).

Open a pack, tap ▶ to listen and + ADD to put it on the timeline. Every sound has a short description of what it sounds like.

Searching ​

Type what you want to hear, in your own words — one word or a whole sentence:

  • "door" · "someone slamming the door shut" · "footsteps on snow" · "laser gun" · "a happy win tune"

Hindi words in English letters work too:

  • "bada dhamaka" · "darwaza band" · "jeet" · "paisa"

The best matches come first.

The sound packs are by Kenney (kenney.nl) and are public domain, so you can use them in anything you make, even videos you earn money from.

Voice maker ​

Type a line, pick a Voice (Normal, Kid, Deep, Squeaky, Sleepy) and a Language (English or Hindi), tap Preview to hear it and Add to timeline.

Record your voice ​

Tap Record audio, allow the microphone the first time, then hold the button to record and release to stop. Switch on Play animation while recording to speak in time with your drawings. Recording starts at the current frame.

Animate to the beat ​

Turn on Sound while animating in App settings → Sound (it is off until you do). Then:

  • Each frame's sound plays as you step to it or drag the frame strip, so you can hear exactly where a word or a hit lands.
  • A pink note sits above every frame a beat falls on — a drum hit, a clap, a plucked note. Put your key poses there.
  • The sound wave stays on screen while you play the animation: the wave going by (already played in pink), a dot on every beat, and the playhead in the middle lighting up as a beat passes. The wave button in the top bar, next to the diamond, hides or shows it.
