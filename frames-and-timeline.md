# Frames, onion skin and the timeline

An animation is a row of frames. Each frame is one drawing; played quickly one after another, they
move.

## The frame strip

The thumbnails above the bottom bar are your frames. The current one is in the middle with a pink
border.

- **Tap** a frame to go to it.
- **Drag the strip** (or the pink handle above it) to scrub through the animation. A readout shows
  the current frame, the total and the seconds.
- **Pink +** — adds a new, empty frame after the current one.
- **▶** plays the animation, **⏮ / ⏭** jump to the first and last frame. Tap the screen to stop.

## Frame actions

**Long-press a frame** to open its actions.

![Frame actions](https://getsettalk.github.io/toonik-docs/screenshots/frame-actions.webp)

- **Hold** − / + — show this drawing for longer (up to 48 frames) without drawing it again.
- **Duplicate**, **Delete**, **Copy**, **Paste before**, **Paste after**, **Move left**, **Move
  right**, **Share** (as a picture).
- Tap **Done**, or anywhere outside the bar, to close it.

## Onion skin

Onion skin shows faint copies of the frames before and after, so you can line up your next drawing.
It is on for new projects and never shows in your movie.

![Onion skin on the canvas](https://getsettalk.github.io/toonik-docs/screenshots/onion-skin-stage.webp)

Change it in **⋮ → Onion skin → Edit**:

![Onion skin settings](https://getsettalk.github.io/toonik-docs/screenshots/onion-skin.webp)

- **Colored** — earlier frames red, later frames green.
- **Looping** — the last frame also shows the first, for animations that loop.
- **Frames before / after** — how many neighbours to show (0–10).
- **Transparency before / after** and **Skip frames**.

## Grid

**⋮ → Grid** draws guide lines over the canvas to help with spacing. **Edit** sets the line spacing
and how strong they are. The grid is never in your movie.

## The full timeline

Tap **TIMELINE** at the top for a bigger view of every frame, drawn to scale, with your layers and
audio.

![The timeline](https://getsettalk.github.io/toonik-docs/screenshots/timeline.webp)

- **Tap** a frame to go to it.
- **Drag the pink grip** on the edge of the current frame to hold it longer or shorter.
- **Long-press** a frame to select it; keep dragging to move it. Tap more to select several.
- **Pinch** to zoom the timeline.
- The **Hold** card at the bottom left changes the selected frame's hold; the preview on the right
  plays the animation.

![Several frames selected](https://getsettalk.github.io/toonik-docs/screenshots/timeline-select.webp)

### Groups and loops

Select some frames and tap **Group**. The group can now be repeated with **Loop** — great for a walk
cycle or a blinking eye that plays several times. **Ungroup** undoes it and **Split** cuts a group
in two.

![A frame group with a loop](https://getsettalk.github.io/toonik-docs/screenshots/timeline-group.webp)

The bar at the bottom also has **Undo**, **Redo**, **Select / Deselect**, **Duplicate**, **Copy**,
**Paste**, **Delete** and **Layer**. Tap **TIMELINE** again (or ✕ on the preview) to close it.

## All frames at once

**⋮ → Frames** shows every frame in a grid. Tap one to open it, long-press to select (then
duplicate, delete, copy, paste or share), or long-press and drag to move.

![Frames viewer](https://getsettalk.github.io/toonik-docs/screenshots/frames-viewer.webp)

## Speed and timing

- **Frames per second** (Project settings) sets the speed of the whole animation. 12 fps is a good
  start; 24 fps looks like film; 4–8 fps looks choppy on purpose.
- **Timing** — *On twos* holds every drawing for two frames, so you draw half as many for the same
  length. Hold single frames from the frame actions or the timeline.
