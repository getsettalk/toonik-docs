# Images, video, objects and drawing outside the canvas

## Add an image or a video

Tap **Image/Video** in the bottom bar.

![Add image or video](https://getsettalk.github.io/toonik-docs/screenshots/import-media.webp)

- **Photos** — pick one picture, or several at once (up to 30).
- **Videos** — turn a clip into frames.
- **Photo camera** / **Video camera** — take a photo or record a clip right now.
- **File** — from Google Drive, Downloads or any other app.

One picture lands on the canvas as a selection: move it, resize it, **Crop** it, then tap outside to
put it down.

When you pick **several pictures**, Toonik asks:

- **One per frame** — each picture on a frame of its own. Great for turning photos into a
  stop-motion animation.
- **All on this frame** — side by side on the current frame, to arrange together.

### Importing a video

![Import video](https://getsettalk.github.io/toonik-docs/screenshots/video-import.webp)

- Drag the ends of the bar to choose the part of the clip (**Start** and **End**).
- **Import as** — **Animation** (many frames) or **Single frame** (one still picture).
- **Look** — **Original**, **Line art** (turns the video into drawn lines; choose **Clean**,
  **Medium** or **Rich** detail) or **Trace guide** (a faint, locked layer with an empty "Ink" layer
  on top, so you can trace over the video).
- **Timing** — **On ones** or **On twos**.

The video goes on a new layer, starting at the current frame. Long clips make many frames, so pick
just the part you need.

## Draw outside the canvas

New projects have **room around the canvas**: draw a character off-screen and walk it in, or draw a
wide background for a camera move. Only what is inside the canvas ends up in your movie.

![Drawing in the room around the canvas](https://getsettalk.github.io/toonik-docs/screenshots/draw-outside.webp)

Zoom out to see how much room there is — the dashed line is its edge:

![Zoomed out](https://getsettalk.github.io/toonik-docs/screenshots/draw-outside-zoomed.webp)

Turn it on or off in **⋮ → Project settings → Draw outside the canvas**. Turning it off crops
everything drawn outside the canvas from every frame (you will be asked first).

### Make the room bigger or smaller

While it is on, **Room size** under the switch sets how much room there is, by its width and height:

![Room size](https://getsettalk.github.io/toonik-docs/screenshots/room-size.webp)

- Drag **Width** or **Height**, or tap **−** and **+** to move each side by 10 px. **Automatic** goes
  back to the size Toonik picks; **Current** to what you have now.
- The preview shows your drawing staying where it is, the new edge in pink and, in red, anything
  that would be cut off. You are asked before anything is cut.
- Each layer of each frame is one picture this size, so room uses memory: **Memory** shows how much.
  When one side reaches what your device allows, make the other smaller to go further. Phones and
  tablets with more memory get more room.
- Tap **Apply**. Changing the room can't be undone and clears Undo.

## The Object library

Ready-made drawings and animations you can drop into any project, plus everything you save yourself.
Open it from the **pink diamond** in the editor or **Objects** on the home screen.

![Object library](https://getsettalk.github.io/toonik-docs/screenshots/object-library.webp)

- **Built-in objects** — walk and run cycles, a bouncing ball, a jumping person, a fox, a flapping
  bird, people and animals seen from the front, back and sides (dog, cat, bird, rabbit, panda, fish),
  trees, bamboo, rocks, clouds, sun, moon, stars, a house, a beach ball and a car.
- **Filters** — All, Static, Animated, Favourites, Built-in. **🔍** searches by name or tag.
- Tap an object for its details, to add it to your **favourites**, or to **duplicate** a built-in one
  so you can change it.

![Object details](https://getsettalk.github.io/toonik-docs/screenshots/object-detail.webp)

From the editor the library opens as **Insert object** — tap an object to add it:

![Insert object](https://getsettalk.github.io/toonik-docs/screenshots/insert-object.webp)

When you add an object to a project you can:

- **Place** — put one frame of it on the canvas as a selection.

  ![Place an object](https://getsettalk.github.io/toonik-docs/screenshots/insert-object-place.webp)

- **Stamp** (animated objects) — draw its whole animation across your frames, repeated as many times
  as you like, on the current layer or a new one.

  ![Stamp an animation](https://getsettalk.github.io/toonik-docs/screenshots/insert-object-stamp.webp)

- **Animate** — move it across the canvas with a motion you choose, like *Animate selection*.

### Save your own objects

Select a drawing with the lasso (or use a whole layer, or a range of frames), then **⋮ → Save as
object**. Give it a name and tags so it is easy to find later.
