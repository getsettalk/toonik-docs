# Getting started

Toonik is a frame-by-frame animation app. You draw a picture, add a new frame, draw it again a
little differently, and when the frames play one after another your drawing moves. Everything in
the app is free, there is no account to create and no ads.

## The home screen

![The home screen with four projects](https://getsettalk.github.io/toonik-docs/screenshots/home.webp)

- **Projects** and **Movies** — the two tabs. Swipe left or right to switch.
- **☰ (menu)** — opens the side menu (see below). A pink dot means there is something new to read.
- **🔔 (bell)** — your notifications from Toonik. A number shows how many are unread.
- **Pink circle (top right)** — opens Settings.
- **Bottom bar** — **Home**, **Objects** (the object library), **+** (new project), **Learn** (short
  animation lessons) and **Settings**.

Tap a project to open it. The small labels on each card show how long the animation is and its
frames per second (fps).

### The side menu

![The side menu](https://getsettalk.github.io/toonik-docs/screenshots/home-menu.webp)

**App settings**, **Object library**, **How to animate** (lessons), **What's new** (what each update
brought), **Notifications**, **About**, **Rate this app** and **Share Toonik**.

## Create a project

Tap the pink **+** at the bottom.

![New project screen](https://getsettalk.github.io/toonik-docs/screenshots/new-project.webp)

1. **Project name** — type a name, or keep "My Animation 1".
2. **Choose background** — the four buttons under the preview:
   - **Presets** — paper, white, grid, lined, dot grid, sky, sunset, night sky, meadow, green
     screen, black and more.
   - **Camera** — take a photo and draw over it.
   - **Gallery** — use a picture from your phone.
   - **Color** — a plain colour (pick a swatch or type a HEX code).

   ![Background presets](https://getsettalk.github.io/toonik-docs/screenshots/background-presets.webp)

3. **Choose canvas size** — ready-made sizes for YouTube, YouTube Shorts, Instagram, TikTok,
   stickers and more, or type your own width and height (64 to 2048 pixels).

   ![Canvas sizes](https://getsettalk.github.io/toonik-docs/screenshots/canvas-size.webp)

4. **Choose frames per second** — how many drawings play each second. **12 fps** is a good start.
   The bouncing ball shows how fast it will look.

   ![Frames per second](https://getsettalk.github.io/toonik-docs/screenshots/fps.webp)

5. Tap **Create project**.

> **Good to know:** the canvas size cannot be changed after the project is created. The name,
> speed and background can be changed any time in **Project settings**.

## Work with your projects

**Long-press** a project to select it. Tap more cards to select several.

![Selecting a project](https://getsettalk.github.io/toonik-docs/screenshots/home-select.webp)

The bar at the bottom then lets you:

- ✏️ **Rename** (one project at a time)
- ⧉ **Duplicate** — makes a copy called "… copy"
- **Share / Export project** — saves the whole project as a `.flippa` file you can send, back up or
  open on another phone
- 🗑 **Delete** — asks first, and cannot be undone

Tap **✕** at the top to leave selection mode.

### Sort, import and select

Tap **⋮** next to the tabs.

![Sort and import menu](https://getsettalk.github.io/toonik-docs/screenshots/home-sort.webp)

- **Sort by** — Last modified, Name or Date created.
- **Import project** — open a Toonik backup (`.flippa`) or a **FlipaClip project (`.fc`)**. It is
  added as a new project; nothing you already have is replaced. From FlipaClip, the layers and
  drawings come across (layer effects and sound do not).
- **Select** — start selection mode.

### Time-lapse recording

Toonik can record every stroke you draw and turn it into a time-lapse video of your drawing.
Select a project, tap **⋯** at the top, and switch on **Time-Lapse Recording**. Later, use **Share
Time-Lapse** from the same place. Time-lapse videos also appear in the **Movies** tab.

![Project options](https://getsettalk.github.io/toonik-docs/screenshots/project-options.webp)

## Your work is saved automatically

Toonik saves while you draw (every 10 seconds by default — you can change this in Settings) and
again when you leave the editor or switch to another app. Your projects stay on your phone; nothing
you draw is uploaded anywhere.

## What's new and notifications

**What's new** (side menu) lists every update and what it changed. The **bell** keeps the latest 15
messages from Toonik, even ones you missed. Tap a message to open its link.
